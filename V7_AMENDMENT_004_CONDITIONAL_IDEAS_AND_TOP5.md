# V7 Amendment 004 - Conditional ideas under Rules 24/32, and a top-5 weekly list

**Status:** IN FORCE by owner decision (Elijah Agom, 2026-11-12).
**Effective:** Week 47 (cutoff Sun 15 Nov 2026) and every later weekly file, until a numbered amendment or protocol revision supersedes it.
**Nature:** documented exceptions and tool-behaviour clarifications (Rule 20: deliberate, versioned change). Rule text is not edited.

## Why this exists
Weeks 42, 43, 45 and 46 published no ideas and Week 44 published one. The 2026-11-12 scan of 29 subjects returned WAIT for every pair, most on timing or structure conditions. The confluence report added gates that v7 does not contain (a 3-idea cap, one idea per cluster, UNCLEAR surprise dots vetoing an idea). Macro Engine spec A1 says macro ranks and never excludes or gates a pair.

## 1. Conditional-Transition ideas (exception to Rules 24 and 32)
A pair whose Daily is CONFLICT may be published as a Conditional idea when ALL hold:
1. 4H structure is clean in one direction.
2. Macro or DXY evidence agrees with that direction.
3. The idea states a 4H-close trigger at a named level, a stop (Rule 4), a target tied to a liquidity pool (Rule 33) and R:R (Rule 35).
Labels: HTF Trend: Transition. Tier: lowest (two stars). The trigger is the "stronger confirmation" Rule 24 requires. Rule 26 is unchanged: a countertrend idea still needs a confirmed structural break.

## 2. Pullback inside a clean Daily trend (clarification of Rule 25)
When Daily is clean and 4H is moving against it, the idea is Conditional, with the trigger being 4H structure resuming the Daily direction. Label: With-trend, QMR Phase: Quality or Manipulation. No countertrend label is used.

## 3. Weekly list size
The Trade Priority List holds up to 5 ranked ideas. There is no cluster cap. Rule 5 stays in full: correlated ideas are declared plainly as one family. Do not fill slots (Generation Prompt rule 18).

## 4. Tools are advisory
The confluence report and engine ranking are inputs (Generation Prompt, Inputs). WAIT, UNCLEAR and missing-record flags are printed as warnings. They do not remove an idea that meets Rules 24-35 and this amendment.

## 5. Authorship (clarifies the Implementation Directive decision record)
An AI session may draft the weekly file, daily updates and week-end review under Generation Prompts v7.1, using engine and evidence output as inputs. A draft becomes research only when the owner reviews it and imports it through the admin importer. No cron, script or AI route writes research_cycles or trade_ideas on its own. Engine output (rankings, provisional levels) is advisory input to the draft and is never persisted as a trade idea. This does not change the decision record: Track A analyst-authored, parser-imported research remains the only path that writes a trade idea to the database.

## Not changed
Rules 1-4 (no fabrication, sourcing separation, status from price action, stop required), Rule 26, Rule 35, Amendment 003, and the rule that entry, stop and target inferred from charts are labelled as inferred technical reads (Rule 2).
