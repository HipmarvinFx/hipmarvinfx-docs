# HipMarvin FX — Standing Protocol v7

**v7 — Higher-Timeframe Trend + QMR/QM execution upgrade · 2026-08-23**

v7 preserves the v6 Liquidity/Flow Regime framework and adds a higher-timeframe execution doctrine built around **QMR (Quality → Manipulation → Reaction)** with **QM/QML as an execution refinement**, not a standalone reversal pattern.

## v7 governing principle

> **Trade with the prevailing higher-timeframe trend by default. Countertrend trades are permitted only after a confirmed higher-timeframe structural break. Patterns never override structure.**

## Canonical hierarchy

**Macro Regime → Weekly Thesis → HTF Trend/Structure → 20D Structural Location → Liquidity Map → QMR → Displacement/Acceptance or Rejection → QM/QML Entry Refinement → Liquidity Target → Risk.**

The 20D range remains a location map. Liquidity identifies the likely destination. QMR describes the setup sequence. Higher-timeframe structure determines whether continuation or reversal is eligible. QM/QML refines execution; it does not create directional bias by itself.

## CROSS-PHASE RULES

**Rule 20 — Parser integrity.** Research files remain parser-visible canonical records. Deliberate, versioned schema changes are permitted; silent structural drift is not.

### Rule 21 — 20D Zone / Structural Location

Calculate the most recent 20-trading-day High/Low backward from the most recent Friday.

`zone % = (entry − 20D Low) / (20D High − 20D Low) × 100`

Labels: 0–20% Discount; 20–40% Lower Equilibrium; 40–60% Equilibrium; 60–80% Mild Premium; 80–100% Deep Premium.

The 20D zone is **location only**. Premium does not automatically mean sell. Discount does not automatically mean buy. A directional market may continue through premium or discount.

### Rule 22 — Thesis invalidation flag

If new research materially contradicts the analytical basis for an OPEN position, record the contradiction in Notes with a pointer to the research. It does not by itself change Status, stop, or close the position.

### Rule 23 — Liquidity/Flow Regime

Classify each new Trade Priority List idea as RANGE, TRANSITION, or DIRECTIONAL.

- **RANGE:** liquidity sweeps are repeatedly rejected and price returns into established range.
- **TRANSITION:** meaningful sweep/displacement exists but acceptance or continuation is incomplete/conflicting.
- **DIRECTIONAL:** liquidity is consumed sequentially, displacement has follow-through, and broken liquidity/structure is accepted.

Liquidity State: `Untaken liquidity`, `Liquidity swept + rejected`, `Liquidity swept + accepted`, `Sequential liquidity consumption`, `Transition / confirmation pending`.

### Rule 24 — Higher-Timeframe Trend Doctrine

The system is **trend-following by default**.

Required analytical timeframes:

- **Daily:** broad structural regime and major swing structure.
- **4H:** primary actionable trend and structural bias.
- **1H:** minimum execution timeframe and setup confirmation.

**No directional or execution decision may be based on a timeframe below 1H.** 30M, 15M, 5M and lower are excluded from the QMR execution model.

The default direction must agree with the prevailing Daily/4H structure. If Daily and 4H disagree, classify the market as **TRANSITION / CONFLICT** and require stronger confirmation; do not manufacture a directional bias from the lower timeframe.

### Rule 25 — Trend Continuation Is the Default

If the HTF structure is bullish, prefer longs. If bearish, prefer shorts.

The following are **not sufficient reasons to reverse the bias**:

- Premium or Discount location
- 20D range extension
- a previous high/low being touched
- a liquidity wick/sweep by itself
- a single rejection candle
- a textbook-looking QM pattern
- apparent overextension

A countertrend idea is rejected unless Rule 26 is satisfied.

### Rule 26 — Countertrend Eligibility Requires Structural Break

A reversal/countertrend trade becomes eligible only after a **meaningful higher-timeframe structural break** against the prevailing trend.

A valid break requires, where applicable:

1. A clearly identified protected HTF swing or structural level.
2. Price to break that level with decisive displacement/acceptance rather than a mere wick.
3. Evidence that the break is being held or followed through.
4. A subsequent QMR reaction or retest that supports the new direction.

A wick through structure followed by immediate reclamation is **not** a confirmed trend reversal.

### Rule 27 — QMR Setup Model

QMR means:

**Q — Quality:** identify the relevant HTF location, liquidity, structural level and macro context.

**M — Manipulation:** observe the liquidity interaction or pullback that creates the opportunity. A sweep is evidence, not an automatic reversal signal.

**R — Reaction:** require actual price response: displacement, acceptance/rejection, and/or structural confirmation.

For continuation, QMR should normally occur **with the prevailing HTF trend**.

For reversal, QMR is eligible only after Rule 26 confirms structural break.

### Rule 28 — QM/QML Is an Entry Refinement, Not a Bias Engine

QM/QML may be used to refine the entry after the directional thesis and QMR reaction are already valid.

Do not create a trade solely because a chart resembles a Quasimodo pattern.

The preferred sequence is:

`HTF trend → quality location → liquidity interaction → displacement/structural reaction → QM/QML retracement → entry → next liquidity target.`

A non-textbook structural reaction remains valid if the underlying sequence is present. Pattern symmetry is secondary to structure.

### Rule 29 — Continuation Through Premium/Discount Is Allowed

A Buy in Premium or Sell in Discount is not automatically invalid.

Continuation is justified when:

- HTF trend agrees with the trade;
- liquidity has been accepted rather than rejected;
- displacement has follow-through;
- structure is holding in the trade direction; and
- a credible next liquidity target exists.

This rule preserves the v6 lesson from GBP/USD and JPY examples: location can be extended while directional flow remains intact.

### Rule 30 — Trend Extension / 20D Range Discipline

A move beyond the normal 20D range does not create a mean-reversion trade by itself.

If HTF trend and liquidity flow remain directional, continuation remains eligible. If flow loses acceptance and meaningful structure breaks, reassess under Rules 26–28.

### Rule 31 — No Pattern Override

No candlestick formation, QM shape, order block, imbalance, premium/discount label, or other pattern may override:

**Macro context → HTF structure → liquidity/flow → confirmed reaction.**

Patterns are supporting evidence only.

### Rule 32 — No Trade When HTF Structure Is Unclear

If Daily/4H structure is conflicting, choppy, or insufficiently defined, the default action is **WAIT / NO TRADE** unless a documented transition setup satisfies the required confirmation.

### Rule 33 — Liquidity Target Discipline

Targets should be tied to credible opposing or next liquidity pools. Do not use a fixed target merely because it produces a preferred R:R.

### Rule 34 — Required Trade Direction Fields

Every Priority List idea must explicitly state:

- **HTF Trend:** Bullish / Bearish / Transition / Conflict
- **Trend Alignment:** With-trend / Countertrend after structural break / Not eligible
- **Structural Break:** None / Confirmed bullish / Confirmed bearish / Not applicable
- **QMR Phase:** Quality / Manipulation / Reaction / Confirmed continuation / Confirmed reversal
- **QM/QML Refinement:** Yes / No / Not applicable
- **Execution TF:** 1H / 4H / Daily

A trade marked `Countertrend after structural break` must cite the broken HTF structure and confirmation evidence in Reasoning.

### Rule 35 — No Automatic Trade From Any Label

DIRECTIONAL, QMR, QM, premium/discount, or liquidity classification never creates an entry by itself. Every trade still requires a defined level, stop, target, risk/reward, timeframe and valid thesis/context.
